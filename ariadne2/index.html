Ariadne2: Reinforcement Learning for Single-Robot Autonomous Exploration

A learning-based planner for selecting informative frontier viewpoints in unknown occupancy-grid environments while respecting low-level motion constraints.

Earlier research projectMARMoT Laboratory, NUS

Overview

Ariadne2 formulates autonomous exploration as a graph-structured sequential decision problem over dynamic frontier viewpoints. The system combines frontier clustering, Gaussian viewpoint proposal, Transformer encoding, pointer-style action selection, and A*-constrained execution.

Training uses Soft Actor-Critic with expert-aligned rewards and a Ray-based distributed rollout pipeline, connecting high-level information gain with feasible robot motion in the current map.

Approach

At each decision step, the planner proposes and groups frontier viewpoints, estimates their newly visible area, and encodes the candidates with the current map state. A pointer-style policy selects the next viewpoint; A* constrains its execution to traversable paths. Expert-aligned rewards combine exploration coverage and travel distance during Soft Actor-Critic training.
